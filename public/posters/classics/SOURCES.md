The documented rights basis is United States public domain, not a worldwide copyright clearance. Commons notes that some editions may remain protected in jurisdictions that do not apply the rule of the shorter term. Source links are retained for jurisdiction-specific review. No attribution is required by these public-domain designations; creator and provenance details are retained here for traceability.

The previously supplied poster files in the parent directory are preserved and are not used by the landing-page letters or grid.
